**Design placeholder — not a published privacy policy. Verify before merging.**

- What data does the hosting provider or CDN receive in ordinary requests,
  including IP addresses and access logs? Which provider actually serves the
  deployed site, and what are its retention and processing practices?
- Are analytics, cookies, local storage, embedded media, third-party scripts,
  forms, or comments present on the deployed site? Check the real response,
  not just repository configuration.
- Do RSS subscriptions or outbound links send readers to other services?
  Explain any relevant third-party handling without claiming control over it.
- If a contact route is added, what information does it collect and how long
  is it kept?
- Who should readers contact about privacy questions, and which legal
  requirements apply to the site's audience and location?

Write only what you have verified. If a formal privacy notice is unnecessary,
this draft can be removed instead of published.
